Shopping Tips

Before You Buy

  • Consider your child's specific sensory profile (use our Sensory Profile Builder)
  • Start with one or two items to see what works before investing in more
  • Look for return policies in case an item doesn't work for your child
  • Consider involving your child's OT in product selection when possible
  • Read reviews from other parents of 2e children

Budget-Friendly Alternatives

  • DIY Weighted Blanket: Sew pockets into a blanket and fill with rice or poly pellets
  • DIY Sensory Bottles: Use empty water bottles with glitter, beads, or colored oil
  • DIY Fidgets: Paper clips, rubber bands, play-doh, or textured fabrics
  • Free Visual Supports: Download and print our free visual schedules
  • Movement Breaks: No equipment needed - jumping jacks, wall pushes, animal walks
