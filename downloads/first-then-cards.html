FIRST / THEN Cards

How to Use:

  1. Print this page and laminate
  2. Add pictures or words for specific activities
  3. Show: "FIRST [less preferred task], THEN [preferred activity]"
  4. Example: "FIRST homework, THEN iPad time"
  5. Helps motivate task completion and reduce resistance

Tip: Make sure the "THEN" activity is something highly motivating!
