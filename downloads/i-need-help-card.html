I Need Help Card

How to Use:

  • Print and laminate these cards
  • Cut out and place where child can easily access
  • Child can hand card to adult when verbal communication is difficult
  • Especially helpful during meltdowns, sensory overload, or anxiety
  • Teaches non-verbal communication and self-advocacy
