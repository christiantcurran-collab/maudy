⏰ Screen Time Timer

Visual timer for screen time limits

How to Use:

  • Print and laminate the timer card
  • Fill in agreed-upon screen time duration
  • Cross off or cover each 5-minute block as time passes
  • When all blocks are covered, screen time is over
  • Use a physical timer alongside for audio cue
  • Prepare child with 5 and 2 minute warnings
