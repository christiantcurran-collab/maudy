⭐ Token Reward Chart

Earn tokens for positive behaviors!

How to Use:

  • Fill in the goal behavior at the top
  • Write in the reward at the bottom
  • Child earns one token (sticker/stamp) for each time they complete goal behavior
  • When all 10 boxes are filled, child gets the reward
  • Start with achievable goals and highly motivating rewards
  • Can laminate and use dry-erase markers to reuse
