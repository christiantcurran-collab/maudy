NOW / NEXT Board

How to Use:

  1. Print this page
  2. Laminate for durability (optional)
  3. Add picture cards or write activities in each box
  4. Show child what activity is NOW and what comes NEXT
  5. Update as activities change throughout the day

Tip: Use velcro dots to attach picture cards that can be easily swapped!
